How It Works

📝

1. Submit a Complaint

Select the category, mark the location, choose the ratings, and optionally add a photo.

📍

2. Pinpoint the Problem

Use the map or your device location to identify the affected area.

🎯

3. Smart Priority Ranking

Complaints are ranked using severity, safety risk, and community impact so authorities can identify urgent issues.

🖼️

4. Duplicate Complaint Detection

CivicPulse can compare uploaded photos and locations to help identify repeated reports of the same issue.

⏱️

5. Automatic Report Time

The date and time are recorded automatically when the complaint is submitted.

🔎

6. Complaint Identification

Each saved complaint receives a unique ID, such as CP-0001.
