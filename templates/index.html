How CivicPulse Works

Three simple steps to bring attention to problems in your community.

01

Choose and Report

Select a category, enter the location, describe the impact, and optionally upload a photo.

02

Smart Prioritization

CivicPulse calculates a priority score using severity, safety risk, and estimated community impact.

03

Record and Follow Up

Each saved complaint receives a unique complaint ID and a status for tracking.
